Good to know

Questions? Let’s clear a few things up.

Find answers about buying, shipping and tracking your Globe Link vehicle. If you need a hand, our team is one message away.

How do I purchase a vehicle?

Open a vehicle’s details and add it to your cart, then submit a purchase request. A Globe Link specialist will confirm availability, inspection information, payment arrangements and delivery details before you commit.

Are shipping costs included in the vehicle price?

No. Vehicle prices are listed separately from shipping. Use our shipping estimator for an initial idea; the final quote depends on origin, destination, vehicle dimensions and chosen transport method. Taxes and import duties are also destination-dependent.

How long does delivery take?

Transit time varies by destination and vehicle type. Typical estimates range from 10 to 28 business days after export preparation. Your coordinator will provide a more specific schedule before dispatch.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. After dispatch, we provide a shipment reference and updates through the Track order page. Your shipping coordinator can also help with carrier milestones.

Can I arrange an inspection before buying?

Yes. Contact our team to discuss available inspection reports, documentation and any additional checks for a selected vehicle.

Are registration, taxes and import duties included?

These requirements depend on the destination country and are not included in the listed vehicle price or initial shipping estimate. Buyers should check local regulations; our team can help identify the relevant steps.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment options and terms are confirmed individually by a vehicle specialist after a purchase request. We will share secure payment instructions directly.

How can I contact Globe Link?

Use our contact form or email hello@globelink.example. Our team typically replies within one business day.
